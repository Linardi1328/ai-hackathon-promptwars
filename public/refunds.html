Payments & Refunds

The current Event Twin demo does not sell tickets, event registrations, subscriptions, licences, or other paid services through the website and does not collect payment information.

Effective: 11 September 2026

PrivacyTermsCookiesPayments & refunds

No website checkout

There is no payment gateway, recurring billing, paid registration, ticket purchase, donation flow, or other customer checkout in the audited prototype. Accordingly, there are no Event Twin website purchases to cancel or refund.

Future commercial deployment

If Event Twin later accepts payments, the deployed product must publish accurate seller identity, pricing, taxes/mandatory charges, service description, cancellation/refund rules, statutory consumer rights, payment-provider information, and privacy disclosures before checkout is enabled.

Unexpected payment request

If a page claiming to be this demo unexpectedly asks for card details, banking credentials, cryptocurrency, or another payment, do not proceed unless the official site policies have been updated to describe that feature.
